# cipher-hunt

Working notes for a hunt through history's still-unsolved ciphers. Two deep source pages were collected and mined for every cipher they still list as unsolved:

- Daniel Bourdeau's **"Unsolved Historical Ciphers — Working Notes, Solutions and Corrections"** (dbourdeau.github.io/cyphersolver) — an interactive timeline of 248 catalogue entries drawn from the DECODE historical-ciphers database, with working notes, solution records, and corrections from a Sept–Oct 2026 breaking sprint.
- The **"Unsolved Historical Ciphers"** article on cryptiana.web.fc2.com — a long-form, language-by-language survey by the same research circle (it cites Bourdeau's contributions), marking each item Solved/Unsolved as of Sept–Oct 2026.

The extracted list lives in [notes/unsolved-ciphers.md](notes/unsolved-ciphers.md). Raw page captures with retrieval provenance are under [sources/](sources/PROVENANCE.md).

Sources:
- https://dbourdeau.github.io/cyphersolver/index.html
- https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/unsolved.htm
