Subject: Enquiry re: Egerton MS 1788, RP 9319, Add MS 4186 — Charles I cipher material (1648)

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am researching the unsolved ciphers of Charles I from his 1648
imprisonment on the Isle of Wight, and I am writing to ask about
digitisation or reprographic availability of three items identified
in your catalogues:

1. **Egerton MS 1788, ff. 51, 53, 54** — "Cipher designed by Charles I.,
   with note by the same. Holograph", within the volume of original
   Carisbrooke letters from Charles I to Henry Firebrace (23 Apr.–27 Dec.
   1648), holograph in a feigned hand, signed "J.".

2. **RP 9319** — 70 photocopies of an early-18th-century transcript volume
   (Copies of Exported Manuscripts Deposited under Government Export
   Regulations), containing a copy of 66 secret letters written by
   Charles I and smuggled out of the Isle of Wight, the originals of
   which went into the hands of Hopkins. The transcript mentions "an
   addition of some Figures, with Names" sent 26 July 1648, which may be
   a partial cipher key.

3. **Add MS 4186, f. 80** — a cipher within Thomas Birch's 1764 printer's
   copy of the Hammond/Derby House letters (1647–48).

Could you advise whether any of these are available through Digitised
Manuscripts, and if not, the process and cost for ordering
reprographic copies of the specific folios above?
