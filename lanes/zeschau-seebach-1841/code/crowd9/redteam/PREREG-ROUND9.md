## Standing rules (no exceptions, all work orders)

1. **≥2 independent legs for ANY promotion.** Independent = different cipher cells
   (pre-registered independence definition), different register-matched corpora, or
   different instruments — never two readings of the same datum. One leg, however
   strong, is never promotion-grade. **Hold this line.**
2. **Lane n≥3 kill rule:** a single adverse is fenced, not kill-grade. Kill or demotion
   needs a pre-registered kill condition with an exact test, not post-hoc conjunction.
3. **F33 conditioning:** conditioned readings must be pre-registered (partition rule,
   identity or honest identity-NULL, predicted window list) BEFORE classification.
   Post-hoc partitions — including EXPANSIONS of a banked condition to new cells —
   are not granted. Windows inside a conditioned domain that contradict it are
   adverses, fenced at n<3.
4. **No double-counting:** a datum banked as a leg in round 8 (or earlier) cannot be a
   new leg in round 9. Cite it, don't re-spend it. (Round-8 legs: R1 H1–H6, R2 S1–S5/L1–L5,
   R3 C1–C3/D1–D3, R4 en/noun islet frames, R5 B1/B3/B2a/B2b/B4/B5, R6 WO-10 cipher cell,
   R7 M_hom legs 1–3 + L_A/L_B + 06-islet frames, R8 T1/T2/D1/D2, R9 X1–X4.)
5. **Exact tests, pre-registered bars.** Corpus legs must be era- AND register-matched
   (the 1841-diplomatic-French steer: attempt-3 showed 1830s–40s diplomatic ≠ 1862
   novel French). Report per-file/per-register dispersion; the register-best
   comparator is the bar, not the friendliest file. **Rate bars use Nesselrode v8;**
   the fait 4.6× soft edge applies symmetrically (R2/R3 precedent).
6. **Clean fails are fails.** No rescuing via post-hoc respecification.
7. **NEVER score bearing counts on manual tilings** (T7 standing). Per-window nulls
   only; assert arity on recounts.
8. **No re-litigation of settled kills.** 48="ne"-allophone (F60, KILLED), 93="l'"
   alone (rate-kill), the refuge concretizations (X1–X4 dead), and the retired WO-6
   second-window criterion are CLOSED. A package re-litigating any of them gets an
   **INTERIM KILL** — re-litigation of a settled kill is a standing-rule violation,
   not a disagreement.
9. **F60 (interchangeability necessary≠sufficient).** Contact-interchange surviving
   is not a positive leg for any merger/homophony claim.
10. **F56 (merger template).** Merger claims are tested on the interchangeability
    exact-binomial template with a pre-registered contact pair; p<0.01 REFUTES.
11. Fences stay fenced at n<3. H5/H6-style adverses remain standing instruments.
