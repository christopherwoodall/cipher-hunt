## Standing rules (no exceptions, all twelve work orders)

1. **≥2 independent legs for ANY promotion.** Independent = different cipher cells
   (pre-registered independence definition), different register-matched corpora, or
   different instruments — never two readings of the same datum. One leg, however
   strong, is never promotion-grade. **Hold this line.**
2. **Lane n≥3 kill rule:** a single adverse is fenced, not kill-grade. Kill or demotion
   needs a pre-registered kill condition with an exact test, not post-hoc conjunction.
3. **F33 conditioning:** conditioned readings must be pre-registered (partition rule,
   identity or honest identity-NULL, predicted window list) BEFORE classification.
   Post-hoc partitions are not granted. Windows inside a conditioned domain that
   contradict it are adverses, fenced at n<3.
4. **No double-counting:** a datum banked as a leg in round 7 (or earlier) cannot be a
   new leg in round 8. Cite it, don't re-spend it.
5. **Exact tests, pre-registered bars.** Corpus legs must be era- AND register-matched
   (the 1841-diplomatic-French steer: attempt-3 showed 1830s–40s diplomatic ≠ 1862 novel French).
   Report per-file/per-register dispersion; the register-best comparator is the bar, not the
   friendliest file.
6. **Clean fails are fails.** Own-design-flaw admissions are the model (M1 VOID).
   No rescuing via post-hoc respecification.
