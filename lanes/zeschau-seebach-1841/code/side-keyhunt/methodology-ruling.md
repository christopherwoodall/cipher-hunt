   Naive 1,882-pair concatenation: phase(1532) = EVEN ✓, phase(3453) = ODD ✗.
   Satisfies (i), violates (ii).

   naive concatenation of the 3,764 digits yields exactly 1,882 pairs.

   the exact 12-digit crib substring on a5_03) is rejected: it discards a
   direct manuscript observation to save one statistical EM phase choice,
   and EM-on-pair-frequency is exactly the kind of weak instrument this lane
   has already voided elsewhere (F26).
7. Residual: a transcription length error in rows 0-27 could also explain the
   phase, but the offset flip is the minimal repair (no change to the hand
   transcription). If a future genuine key breaks on the repaired parse,
   revisit — the manuscript images are the final arbiter.
