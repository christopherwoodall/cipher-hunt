## Per-clause pass/fail

**C1 — FAIL.** No 33 window admits "a"+NP with zero new assumptions. The
"a" is the French preposition à (the dative marker of "dire à [NP]").
There is no standing "à" token in the registry (0/40 cells), and no adjacent
token has positional letter-tier evidence for 'a' (letter-tier banked tokens
are 12=n, 34=i, 40=e, 82=m, 48=e; none sits adjacent to 33 as an 'a'
positional). Naming any open neighbor (15/55/01/73/52/16/66/85/37/67/98)
as "à" is a new assumption — the bar's zero-assumption clause excludes it,
and §3 of the protocol bars inventing values. The most frequent frames are
"pour [33] X" x8 (00=pour, not à) and "67 [33] 29" x5 (67/et-veut, no à).
No fused à-bearing unit (A12 37-01, 87-01=ceci) touches 33.

**C2 — FIRES.** The dative avenue is fenced permanently at battery grade:
the cipher has no licensed à-position adjacent to any 33 window under standing
values, so the "dire a [NP]" vs "croire [NP]" asymmetry cannot be landed
through this avenue. This fence is permanent — a future named "à" elsewhere
would open a NEW battery, not re-open this one.

## Verdict rationale

**KILL.** The bar's else-arm is a definitive negative, not an inconclusive
null: the exhaustive census (all 40 cells, all 25 windows, all neighbors)
rejects the avenue at the lane's standard. Per §4, kills regenerate no
follow-ups — the fence closes the work.
