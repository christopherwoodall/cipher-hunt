- Context: WO2 tasked me with the F21 lead — 06 = verb stem (06→77 "pas"

  all 46 occurrences against era verb-continuation expectations, resolving
  companion 67, re-testing 77="pas" vs 77="que", and checking tension with

- Decision: **CONFIRM 06 as verb stem (/mɑ̃/ "demand-/command-") with 4
  independent checks; CONFIRM 67="veut"-class modal; CONFIRM 77="pas";
  REFUTE 77="que" as primary; DISSOLVE the WO1 tension (different 06s)** —
  but do NOT promote 06 to anchor: the group is polyvalent (also /ɑ̃/
  "-ment"), so "06=verb stem" holds per-role, not uniformly.
- Why: the mute-e insight converted the two "anomalies" into confirmations
