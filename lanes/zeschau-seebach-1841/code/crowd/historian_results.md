## 1. Identities — ESTABLISHED

**Sender: Heinrich Anton von Zeschau** (4 Feb 1789, Jessen bei Sorau — 17 Mar 1870, Dresden).
Saxon finance minister Dec 1831–1848; **took over the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in 1835**
(so the cipher letters 1841–43 are minister-to-envoy traffic). Returned to service 1851–69 as
Minister of the Royal House. Biography: v. Witzleben, *Heinrich Anton von Zeschau* (Leipzig 1874).
Sources: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinrich_Anton_von_Zeschau and
https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/ADB:Zeschau,_Heinrich_Anton_von (ADB, Flathe), both retrieved 2026-10-07.
Kalliope lists 161 records under "Zeschau, Heinrich Anton von (1789-1870)" (letters; none described
as cipher material in the indexed snippet):
https://kalliope-verbund.info/de/graph?firstparam=true&fq=ead.pers.index%3A%28%22Zeschau%2C+Heinrich+Anton+von+%281789-1870%29%22%29&lastparam=true

**Recipient: Albin Leo von Seebach** (31 Jan 1811, Langensalza — 16 Jul 1884).
Cadet/junker → Saxon diplomatic service; attaché St Petersburg (Russian speaker); after
Lützerode's recall became **Geschäftsträger 1839, Ministerresident 1840, Envoy 1847**;
Saxon envoy to Russia **1839–1852**, then Paris 1853–1870 (also accredited Brussels),
Turin 1871–75. Married 13 Oct 1839 **Marie von Nesselrode-Ereshofen, daughter of Russian
state chancellor Karl Robert von Nesselrode** (inference: his Russian posting and Nesselrode
connection explain why he held St Petersburg so long). Published *Russische Novellen und
Skizzen* (Leipzig 1837). Raised to Graf 1864 (Belgian king).
Sources: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albin_Leo_von_Seebach,
https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liste_der_s%C3%A4chsischen_Gesandten_in_Russland,
ADB Bd. 33 via https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/downloadPDF?url=sfz74788.pdf,
all retrieved 2026-10-07. Sächsische Biografie (ISGV) also carries an entry (linked from Wikipedia).

- **Contacts:** E-Mail **poststelle@sta.smi.sachsen.de** (general) /
  **poststelled@sta.smi.sachsen.de** (Dresden reading room); Tel **+49 351 89219-710**;

- Suggested order wording: 10731 Nr. 12 (full file), plus 10717 Nr. 3332 (1841) and Nr. 3333
