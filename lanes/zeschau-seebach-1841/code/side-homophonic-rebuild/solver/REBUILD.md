# REBUILD.md — Seebach homophonic solver rebuild (2026-10-07)

Rebuild of `code/side-homophonic/solver/` (frozen, CONTROL-FAIL) addressing
the RUN-REPORT diagnosis D1–D4. The frozen code is untouched; everything
here lives under `code/side-homophonic-rebuild/`. The annealer, moves,
7-pin anchors, phase/Potts machinery, polyvalence E-step, and concentration
penalty are unchanged. Three surgical changes: the S_word scorer (D2), the
S_char length bias (D2b, found during rebuild), λ_poly (D3), and the
Tier-1 inventory (D4).

## 1. New objective formula

```
J = S_char + λ_word·S_word + S_potts + S_soft − λ_poly·n_poly − λ_conc·S_conc
```

- **S_char** = Σ_t F(pcell[t−1], pcell[t]) / len(pcell[t]): per-pair
  length-normalized char-5-gram rate (`solver.py:400-423`). Was the raw
  char sum. See §4.
- **S_word** = S_cov − S_single, λ_word = 1.0 (unchanged weight):
  - **S_cov** = Σ_c max_{lexicon hits h covering c, len(h) ≥ 6} w_h/len_h
    (`solver.py:546-575`). Was the overlapping Aho-Corasick sum. See §2.
  - **S_single** = Σ_t lex_wt[pcell[t]] (unchanged: anti-word-value prior).
- **S_potts**, **S_soft**: unchanged. **λ_poly = 50** (was 20; §5).
  **λ_conc = 5, conc_cap = 6**: unchanged.

## 2. D2 fix — S_word rewrite (`solver.py:237-244`, `546-575`; config `word_minlen`)

**Hole:** the frozen overlapping AC sum counted every overlapping short-word
hit; degenerate repetition of fragments scored S_ac=13,510 vs 779 on the
true decode (R10.5-corrected ratio ~17×).

**Fix (two parts):**
1. **Per-char best-hit, length-normalized, no-overlap** (`_cover_sum`):
   each character is counted at most once, attributed to its best covering
   hit; each hit contributes exactly its lexicon weight w_h spread over its
   characters (w_h/len_h per char). Same lexicon/weights (`lm_ref`,
   3,546 words — R10.1 correction). Exact windowed deltas preserved (a hit
   covering a changed char starts within MAXW of it); `--self-test` passes
   (max err ~2e-10, 300 moves).
2. **Minimum matched length 6** (`word_minlen`, `solver.py:122`,
   `config.json:11`): hits with len < 6 contribute nothing. By-ear chunks
   are 1–4 chars; len ≤ 5 lexicon hits ('meme' 3,962 + 'leur' 1,870 +
   'leurs' 350 + 'kelke' 187 nats on the frozen degenerate decode — 99% of
   its S_cov) are manufacturable from 1–2 fragments. Len ≥ 6 needs ≥ 2
   chunks AND a real French word. The gate key is fail-loud
   (`self.cfg['word_minlen']`, `solver.py:567`): a missing key raises
   instead of silently disabling the gate (the R2 re-score ran with the
   gate inadvertently off — see §7).

**Residual (known limitation):** the frozen degenerate still earns
S_cov=1,305 from one manufactured len-6 word, 'eleurs' ('e'+'leurs',
1,193 nats). Any lexicon method counts real substrings; the defense is the
unchanged S_single (word-values penalized: −1,775 here) plus the
concentration penalty. Net S_word on the frozen degenerate: −470 vs
+211 on truth.

## 4. S_char degeneracy guard — per-pair length normalization (`solver.py:400-423`)

**Found during rebuild** (red-team concern b): after the D2 fix, a 1×2000
pilot anneal under the new objective found a *clean fragment salad*
(values 'r','p','ad','i','re',…; S_single=0, n_poly=11) at total −6,425 —
**1,256 nats above truth** (−7,681). S_word was not the vector; S_char was:
salad −5,841 vs truth −7,593.

**Root cause — length bias:** S_char sums logp over every decode char, so
shorter values earn fewer negative terms. Measured: salad −3.16/pair vs
truth −4.11/pair **at equal per-char rates** (−2.44 vs −2.47 nats/char).
~99% of the 1,752-nat S_char gap is decode length (2,390 vs 3,072 chars),
not French-likeness.

**Fix:** F(ca,cb) now returns the per-pair rate (÷ len(cb)). Each pair's
term is local in t, so exact delta scoring is preserved and the E-step's
choose() now compares readings by per-char quality (length no longer
biases secondary selection). The salad's S_char edge collapses from ~1,750
nats to ~50. Note: S_char's absolute scale shrinks ~0.6×; the annealer runs
slightly colder at the same T0 — the Runner may retune T0.

**Second S_char exploit — E-step selection bias (documented, countered by
λ_poly):** the frozen degenerate's v1-only decode scores −2.71/pair
(WORSE than truth's −2.64); its −1.41/char rate comes entirely from the 60
secondaries letting each occurrence pick the better of two readings
(+2,200 nats, 36.7/secondary). This is inherent to the hard E-step (Viterbi
bias); λ_poly=50 prices it (see §5).

## 5. D3 fix — λ_poly 20 → 50 (`config.json:10`; R4)

**Principled criterion** (not tuned to n_poly=6): λ_poly is the price of a
secondary in nats; a secondary must improve the data likelihood (via the
E-step) by more than λ_poly to be worthwhile. Measured on 184101 under the
new objective:
- 6 genuine polyvalent islets (R5: 94=ne/en, 52=pas/so, 06=-ent/stem…):
  **57.6 nats/secondary** of S_char.
- 60 spurious degenerate secondaries: **36.7 nats/secondary** (average;
  the E-step selection bias documented in §4).

λ_poly=50 sits between: genuine islets (57.6 > 50) survive, spurious
runaway (36.7 < 50) does not pay. It is a statement about per-secondary
information content — "a secondary must explain real alternation, not fit
noise" — not about the count 6.

**Insensitivity (R4):** truth-vs-degenerate margin at λ_poly ∈ {30,50,70}
is +475 / +1,555 / +2,635 nats — truth wins at all three; the verdict does
not hinge on the exact value.

## 10. ADVERSARIAL PILOT (2026-10-07, post-rebuild): the objective is STILL
misaligned — DO NOT RUN the fresh batch on this objective

A 4-restart × 40,000-iter pilot under the FINAL objective (seed 184101,
`--no-soft`; `/tmp/rebuild-pilot-final/result.json`) converged all four
restarts to **−2,376…−2,636**, i.e. **2,577 nats ABOVE the planted truth
(−4,953)**. The winner (restart 199939, npoly=0):

| key | S_char | S_word | poly | conc | total | PRIMARY |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| planted truth | −4,865.2 | +211.4 | −300 (6) | 0 | −4,953.3 | 1.000 |
| pilot morpheme-salad | −3,325.7 | +1,021.9 | 0 (0) | −50 | **−2,376.3** | **0.0112** |

The annealer discovered a **morpheme salad**: values are French morphemes
('tre'×8, 'elle'×7, 'ter'×7, 'pre'×7, 'me'×6, 'gouverne'×6, 'par'×6,
'les'×5, 'pro'×4, 'ment'×4, …; 37 distinct). Decode:
"meideprepluprogreieleurelepropreiieleureleprogreiiileguvernemAke…".
It beats truth on **both** likelihood terms: S_char −1.80/pair vs
−2.64/pair, S_word +1,022 vs +211 (morphemes tile into real words:
'pro'+'pre'='propre', …). Penalties are negligible (n_poly=0, conc −50).

**Root cause — the likelihood itself is misaligned, not its weights.**
The Tocqueville char-5-gram assigns better per-char rates to adversarial
morpheme-salad than to register-gapped, ear-noised real French (Les Mis).
The salad dominates truth on S_char (+1,540) AND S_word (+811); the
structural penalties (poly, conc) cannot cover ~2,600 nats without also
killing truth (projected quota distributions overlap: truth max 6–10
across seeds, salad max 8–9). This is Goodhart on the LM: any "French-
likeness" term the annealer can see, it will out-optimize the fixed truth
with. The D2/D2b/D3/D4 fixes raised the bar (frozen margin was −11,087;
pre-gate −2,630) but the optimum is still degenerate.

**Recommendation:** do not run the fresh 6-instance batch on this
objective — it is expected to converge to morpheme-salad (CONTROL-FAIL).
The method needs a French model that outranks adversarial salad below
real French: options are (a) a word-level model with inferred boundaries,
(b) a neural char LM with longer effective context, or (c) a control
without the Tocqueville→Les-Mis register gap. The 5-gram + lexicon
approach, even repaired, cannot certify this control.
