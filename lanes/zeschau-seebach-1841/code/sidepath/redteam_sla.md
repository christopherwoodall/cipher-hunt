## Verdicts

- **KILL** — the candidate is dead. Record the decisive reason. Never recycled
  in this loop (a killed candidate can only return in a later fleet with
  materially new evidence).
- **PASS** — becomes a new crib (goes to the Skeleton Keeper). Survived with
  ≥1 independent check BEYOND the sieve's two, and contradicts nothing.
- **LEAD** — survives screening but evidence is thin or ambiguous. Goes on the
  watch list; may be re-slid next pass. **When in doubt, mark LEAD, not kill.**
  A wrong kill costs the loop a pass; a wrong lead costs nothing.

## What constitutes a KILL

- **Crib contradiction** (the 06=/mɑ̃/ template, N17): the candidate conflicts
  with any of the 7 ground-truth crib values or the 10 provisional values
  (11=la, 70=pre, 82=m, 34=i, 29=er, 40=e, 46=que; 87=ce, 64=qui, 96=par,
  94=ne, 06=verb-stem-class, 67=veut, 78=me LEAD) in a way that cannot be
  absorbed by established polyvalence (06 /ɑ̃/ vs /mɑ̃/, 94 ne/en islets).
- **Rate fail ≥10× on a calibrated leg** (factor-2 band uncalibrated, F26 —
  the 2× bar does NOT kill; the kill bar is ≥10× on a leg that is otherwise
  sound). Legs 29/82/34 are EXCLUDED from rate legs (inherited calibration).
- **Grammatical impossibility**: a French-grammaticality kill must hold under
  the fleet's established encipherer quirks (mute -e written — 40="e",
  F26; final "-er" stripped, F22; rigid syllabification dead, F30). A kill that
  requires rigid era syllabification or the VOID phase mapping (F26) as a
  meaning instrument does not count.
- **Structural contradiction**: a byte-level conflict with the mid-letter
  back-reference ("la première" = 11-70-82-34-29-40 @pair 1033, F12) or the
  rotation-break boundary signal (rotation itself re-verified chi²=188.3;
  boundary signal usable, phase-as-meaning VOID).
- **Joint contradiction**: the candidate breaks a provisional joint (e.g. the
  dissolved F13/era-binomial lesson — check before claiming a joint kill).

## What constitutes a PASS

- Candidate survives the sieve's two checks AND ≥1 independent check that is
  not recycled from the same computation (different evidence family: rate vs
  positional vs rival-exhaustion vs structural).
- No crib contradiction, no ≥10× rate fail on a calibrated leg, no
  grammatical impossibility, no structural contradiction.
- Rival-exhaustion leg: the N2 "exhaustive inversion" template (era word
  space, candidate is the only n>100 passer) counts as an independent check.

## What gets marked LEAD

- Survives screening but independent checks are thin (n<5 corpus occurrences,
  single-leg support, or leg sits at the edge of the uncalibrated factor-2
  band — 1.89×/1.98× precedent, F28 repair at 1.26×).
- Competing provisional rival alive and un-resolved (e.g. 64="même" vs
  64="qui" template, F31): neither kills the other → LEAD both sides' rivals.
- Any case where the decisive kill check depends on a VOID instrument (phase
  mapping as meaning, 'er'-rate ranking, V29-contaminated legs) → LEAD, never
  KILL.
